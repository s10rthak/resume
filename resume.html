Voting system

Illustration

Built in Python and MySQL

I built a voting system alone in Python and MySQL for my Class 12 Computer Science project, and scored 28 out of 30. Voters register, get an automatically generated ID, log in and vote once, because the program checks a “voted” field in the database before saving a vote. An admin logs in with a password to start or stop voting, add or remove candidates, view results and reset the data. The program creates its own database and five tables the first time it runs. The hardest part was debugging and connecting Python to MySQL.

Laser alarm

Built with an LDR and a transistor

I built a laser-based burglar alarm alone for my Class 12 project, and scored 29 out of 30. A laser pointer shines on an LDR, a light-dependent resistor. When someone blocks the beam, the LDR’s resistance rises, a BC547 transistor switches on, and a buzzer and LED turn on. I wired it on a breadboard with one LDR, four 1kΩ resistors, a transistor, an LED, a buzzer and a 9V battery. In testing, the alarm stayed off while the beam hit the LDR and sounded as soon as the beam was blocked. I was new to electronics and had few components. I burned out LEDs with too much voltage, and when the circuit failed I tested each part one by one to find faulty components and loose connections. Keeping the resistance threshold right was also hard.

Gold

Inter DPS Football, Zonals and Nationals

In Grade 11 I played full back for my school. We won silver at the Inter DPS Zonals in Jaipur, with about 24 teams, and gold at the Nationals in Mumbai, with about 42 teams. The top 4 Zonals teams moved on.

In the Zonals semi-final we beat Bhopal 1-0. Bhopal had won the Zonals 11 times, and our school had finished third only once. I told the seniors we would win and gave the calls to the defence.
